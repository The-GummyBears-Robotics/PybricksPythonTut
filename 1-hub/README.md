# Lesson 1: Hub Basics

Goal: make the hub show something, play a sound, and blink the light.

## Run These First

1. `display_text.py` - show one letter, then scroll text.
2. `light_onoff.py` - turn the hub light on and off in a loop.
3. `simplybeep.py` - make the hub beep.

## Change One Thing

- In `display_text.py`, replace `"Hello, world!"` with your team name.
- In `light_onoff.py`, change the color and the wait time.
- In `simplybeep.py`, change the frequency or duration of the beep.

## Challenge

Make a start-up program for your robot:

- show your team initial
- blink the light three times
- play one short sound

Keep it short enough that another teammate can explain every line.
